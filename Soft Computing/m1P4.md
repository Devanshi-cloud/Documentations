# 8. Operations on Fuzzy Sets

In fuzzy sets, all operations happen using **membership values (0 to 1)**.

---

## Complement (NOT A)

**Formula:**

$$
\mu_{A^c}(x)=1-\mu_A(x)
$$

**Meaning:**  
“How much x does **not** belong to A.”

**Shortcut:**  
Subtract from **1**.

---

## Union (A ∪ B)

**Formula:**

$$
\mu_{A \cup B}(x)=\max(\mu_A(x),\mu_B(x))
$$

**Meaning:**  
Take **maximum**.

**Shortcut:**  
OR operation.

---

## Intersection (A ∩ B)

**Formula:**

$$
\mu_{A \cap B}(x)=\min(\mu_A(x),\mu_B(x))
$$

**Meaning:**  
Take **minimum**.

**Shortcut:**  
AND operation.

---

## Algebraic / Vector Product

**Formula:**

$$
\mu_{A.B}(x)=\mu_A(x)\cdot\mu_B(x)
$$

**Meaning:**  
Multiply values.

---

## Scalar Product

**Formula:**

$$
\mu_{aA}(x)=a\cdot\mu_A(x)
$$

**Meaning:**  
Scale the whole fuzzy set.

---

## Sum (Probabilistic Sum)

**Formula:**

$$
\mu_{A+B}(x)=\mu_A(x)+\mu_B(x)-\mu_A(x)\mu_B(x)
$$

---

## Difference

**Formula:**

$$
A-B=A\cap B^c
$$

**Meaning:**  
Belongs to **A but not B**.

---

## Bounded Sum

**Formula:**

$$
\min(1,\mu_A+\mu_B)
$$

**Meaning:**  
Never exceeds **1**.

---

## Bounded Difference

**Formula:**

$$
\max(0,\mu_A+\mu_B-1)
$$

**Meaning:**  
Never goes below **0**.

---

## Disjunctive Sum (XOR)

**Formula:**

$$
(A^c \cap B)\cup(A \cap B^c)
$$

**Meaning:**  
Belongs to **only one** set.

---

## Cartesian Product

**Formula:**

$$
\mu_{A \times B}(x,y)=\min(\mu_A(x),\mu_B(y))
$$

**Used in:**  
Fuzzy relations.

**Shortcut:**  
Take minimum everywhere.

---

## Equality

**Formula:**

$$
A=B \iff \mu_A(x)=\mu_B(x)
$$

**Meaning:**  
All membership values must be same.

---

## Power of Fuzzy Set

**Formula:**

$$
A^\alpha=(\mu_A(x))^\alpha
$$

**If:**
- $\alpha < 1$ → **Dilation** (wider)
- $\alpha > 1$ → **Concentration** (narrow)

# 9. Properties of Fuzzy Sets

---

## Commutative

$$
A \cup B = B \cup A
$$

$$
A \cap B = B \cap A
$$

**Meaning:**  
Order doesn’t matter.

---

## Associative

$$
A \cup (B \cup C) = (A \cup B) \cup C
$$

**Meaning:**  
Grouping doesn’t matter.

---

## Idempotent

$$
A \cup A = A
$$

$$
A \cap A = A
$$

**Meaning:**  
Same set gives same set.

---

## Involution

$$
(A^c)^c = A
$$

**Meaning:**  
Double complement returns original.

---

## De Morgan’s Law

$$
(A \cap B)^c = A^c \cup B^c
$$

$$
(A \cup B)^c = A^c \cap B^c
$$

**Important:** Very important law.

# 10. Real-Life Applications

---

## Weather Classification 🌦️

Categories:
- Cold
- Hot
- Pleasant
- Extreme

Uses:
- Complement → not cold
- Union → extreme
- Intersection → pleasant

Applications:
- AC systems
- Smart thermostats

---

## Netflix Recommendation 🎬

Inputs:
- Mood
- Genre
- Watch history

Uses:
- AND → Intersection
- OR → Union
- NOT → Complement

Output: Personalized recommendations.

---

## Ride Pricing 🚕

Inputs:
- Traffic
- Demand
- Driver availability

Output: Dynamic pricing / surge pricing.

Examples:
- Uber
- Ola

---

## Smartwatch Health ⌚

Inputs:
- Heart rate
- Sleep
- Activity

Output: Health alerts and recommendations.

Example:
- Apple Watch
