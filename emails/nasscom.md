## 1. NASSCOM - Organisational Snapshot  

NASSCOM is India’s premier technology trade body, governed by an **Executive Council** that steers strategy, policy advocacy and ecosystem programmes [1].  The **President** is **Debjani Ghosh**, who has led the association since 2018 and is the primary point of contact for strategic partnerships [2].  NASSCOM operates through several specialised arms:

* **NASSCOM AI** - the “voice of the AI ecosystem”, delivering research, policy, and industry-wide programmes [3]  
* **NASSCOM DeepTech Club** - India’s largest deep-tech startup community, providing mentorship, non-dilutive funding and access to corporate partners [4]  
* **State-level AI Missions** (e.g., Telangana’s **T-AIM**) that blend government support with NASSCOM-run R&D and talent-upskilling [5]

These entities collaborate with **start-ups, investors, academia and government** to create a “vibrant AI ecosystem” [3] and to position India as a global AI hub [6].

## 2. Flagship AI & Deep-Tech Initiatives (2024-2026)

| Initiative | Core Objective | Typical Eligibility |
|------------|----------------|---------------------|
| **AI-Code Sarathi** - a mentorship-driven accelerator for AI product teams [6] | Build scalable AI solutions; provide cloud & IP support | Early-stage AI/ML startups with a working prototype |
| **NASSCOM Gamechangers Award** - grand-challenge competition [6] | Recognise breakthrough AI applications across sectors | Open to Indian AI innovators; DPIIT-recognised firms get priority |
| **DeepTech Club Launchpad** - non-dilutive incubation [7] | Funding, mentorship, access to corporate pilots for deep-tech domains (AI/ML, robotics, quantum, etc.) | Private Ltd or LLP, Indian-registered, ≤10 years old, product-stage MVP |
| **AI Data Labs (30 labs, 570-lab network)** - AI-skill hubs under the **FutureSkills** initiative [8] | Upskill talent on India-specific foundational models | Open to students, professionals, and start-ups; DPIIT-certified entities receive fast-track enrolment |
| **T-AIM (Telangana AI Mission)** - sector-focused AI pilots [5] | Deploy AI in critical sectors (health, agriculture, infrastructure) | Companies with demonstrable AI use-cases; DPIIT-recognised startups enjoy preferential R&D grants |

Across these programmes, **DPIIT-certified startups** receive **priority evaluation, streamlined onboarding, and access to government-backed funding** (e.g., the FutureSkills grant) [8].

## 3. Eligibility, Application & Incentives for DPIIT-Certified Start-ups  

1. **Eligibility** - Most NASSCOM AI and DeepTech schemes require:  
   * Legal registration as a **Private Limited** company or **LLP** in India (no DPIIT registration mandatory, but strongly preferred) [9]  
   * **Age ≤ 10 years** (DeepTech Club) [7]  
   * A **minimum viable product (MVP)** or demonstrable AI/DeepTech prototype [4]

2. **Application Process** -  
   * Submit an online form via the **NASSCOM Accreditation portal**; the platform validates DPIIT certification automatically [9]  
   * Applications are reviewed **monthly**; shortlisted teams present to an expert jury within 2-3 weeks [4]

3. **Incentives for DPIIT-certified firms** -  
   * **Tax holiday under Section 80-IAC** and **self-certification** for labour/environmental laws [10]  
   * **Fast-tracked patent filing rebates (≈ 80 % off)** and priority examination [10]  
   * **Access to the Fund of Funds for Start-ups (FFS)**, collateral-free loans up to ₹10 crore, and seed-grant schemes up to ₹20 lakh [10]  
   * Within NASSCOM programmes, DPIIT status unlocks **dedicated mentorship tracks, pilot-partner matching, and reduced fees for AI-Data Labs** [8].
