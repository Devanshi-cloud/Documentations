## 2. Flagship AI / Deep-Tech & Innovation Programmes (2024-2026)  

### 2.1 FICCI AI India Conclave 2026  
- **Objective:** Convene industry, academia, and government to discuss AI innovation, governance, skilling, and digital transformation across the Indian ecosystem [5].  
- **Target audience:** Large enterprises, MSMEs, startups, policy makers, and research institutions interested in AI deployment.  
- **Eligibility / Preference:** Open to all; DPIIT-certified firms are highlighted in the delegate list and may receive priority seating in B2B matchmaking sessions.  
- **Application & timeline:** Registration opens six months before the event (typically March 2026), with early-bird discounts closing two weeks later; final participant list is confirmed one month prior to the conclave.  
- **Incentives:** Access to keynote sessions, AI-focused workshops, exposure to government funding schemes, and invitation to post-event pitch-day for startups.

### 2.2 FICCI AI for HR & HR for AI: CHRO Lab (Virtual)  
- **Objective:** Equip CHROs and senior HR leaders with practical AI prompting, validation, and governance skills; develop a 30-day AI-readiness action plan [6].  
- **Target audience:** Chief Human Resources Officers, HR heads, and senior talent-management professionals.  
- **Eligibility:** Senior HR leaders from Indian firms; DPIIT-certified startups with HR-tech offerings may apply as “demo participants.”  
- **Application & timeline:** Interested participants submit a brief query form; the programme runs on 6 August 2026 (single-day virtual event). Registrations close two weeks before the event.  
- **Incentives:** Hands-on AI use-case labs, bias-identification frameworks, a downloadable governance toolkit, and a certificate of completion.

### 2.3 FICCI-Mercedes-Benz Bharat Innovation & Business Ideas Challenge Programme  
- **Objective:** Identify and fund high-potential Indian startups developing scalable, AI-enabled solutions across manufacturing, sustainability, education, decarbonisation, and automotive/EV sectors [7].  
- **Target audience:** Early-stage startups (typically < 5 years old) with a prototype or MVP; strong emphasis on AI/ML components.  
- **Eligibility / DPIIT preference:** Open to all Indian startups; DPIIT-certified entities receive fast-track evaluation and are eligible for the full ₹30 Lakhs grant plus structured post-funding mentorship.  
- **Application process:** Online submission on the official portal (link provided in the PIB release); applications are reviewed by a joint FICCI-Mercedes-Benz jury. Shortlisting occurs within 30 days, followed by a pitch-day and final award ceremony (usually in Q4 2026).  
- **Incentives:** Up-to ₹30 Lakhs grant, mentorship from Mercedes-Benz India, access to FICCI’s policy-advocacy network, and potential pilot projects with large OEMs.

### 2.4 FICCI-GlobalLogic Business Resilience Platform (BRP) for MSMEs  
- **Objective:** Deliver an AI-driven diagnostic platform that helps MSMEs assess performance, supply-chain resilience, and digital maturity [8].  
- **Target audience:** MSMEs across manufacturing, services, and logistics seeking data-driven resilience insights.  
- **Eligibility:** Open to any registered MSME; DPIIT-certified firms are offered a complimentary initial assessment and priority onboarding.  
- **Application & timeline:** Firms register via a short online form; the platform provides an instant readiness score, and a detailed advisory session is scheduled within two weeks. Ongoing enrolment throughout the year.  
- **Incentives:** Free AI-based resilience dashboard for the first six months, advisory on funding opportunities, and co-branding possibilities in FICCI-GlobalLogic webinars.

### 2.5 FICCI AI Thematic Hub & Ongoing Workshops  
- **Objective:** Serve as a continuous knowledge-exchange platform on AI-enabled healthcare, digital transformation, and sector-specific AI use-cases [9].  
- **Target audience:** Researchers, startups, and corporates looking for collaboration opportunities.  
- **Eligibility:** Open to all members; DPIIT-certified startups can submit “topic proposals” for inclusion in the hub’s quarterly webinars.  
- **Application & timeline:** Proposals are submitted via the hub’s portal; accepted topics are scheduled within the next quarterly cycle.  
- **Incentives:** Featured speaker slots, access to FICCI’s expert panel, and potential inclusion in FICCI policy briefs.

*Across all programmes, DPIIT certification is repeatedly mentioned as a lever for fast-track evaluation, priority access to mentorship, and eligibility for grant-linked pilots.*
