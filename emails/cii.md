## 1. Organisational Snapshot - Confederation of Indian Industry (CII)

CII is a non-governmental trade association registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, with its headquarters in New Delhi [1]. Its core mission is **policy advocacy, business promotion, and fostering competitiveness across Indian industry**.

- **Director General:** **Chandrajit Banerjee** (in office since May 2008) - the chief executive responsible for overall strategy and external engagement [1].  
- **President (2025-2026):** **Rajiv Memani**, Chairman & CEO, EY India; **President-Designate:** R Mukundan, MD & CEO, Tata Chemicals; **Vice-President:** Dr Suchitra K Ella, Co-founder & MD, Bharat Biotech [1].  
- **Key Supporting Units:**  
  * **CII Technology, Innovation & Research (TIR) Division** - leads the AI, digital, and innovation agenda (contact Namita Bahl, Director-Technology, Innovation & R&D) [2].  
  * **CII Centre of Excellence for Innovation, Entrepreneurship & Start-ups (CII CIES)** - runs accelerator and market-access programmes for startups [3].  
  * **CII-HP Centre for Artificial Intelligence** - a joint industry-academia hub focused on AI adoption in MSMEs .  
  * **Dx-EDGE (Digital Excellence for Growth and Enterprise)** - national initiative to digitise MSMEs, co-led with NITI Frontier Tech Hub and AICTE [4].  
  * **CII-NUS AI Governance Leadership Programme** - a three-day certification for board-level AI governance [5].

Collectively, these bodies give CII a **cross-sectoral innovation platform** that spans large enterprises, MSMEs, and high-growth startups.

## 2. Flagship AI / Deep-Tech & Innovation Programmes (2024-2026)

### a) CII-HP Centre for Artificial Intelligence  
- **Objective:** Accelerate AI adoption in Indian MSMEs through shared infrastructure, training, and pilot projects.  
- **Typical Eligibility:** Indian MSMEs (≤ 250 employees), any sector, with a willingness to adopt AI; DPIIT certification is **highly preferred** because it unlocks additional government incentives.  
- **Application & Timeline:** Companies submit a brief proposal via the CII portal; shortlisting occurs quarterly, with pilots launched within 2-3 months of approval (programme launched at CII Annual Business Summit 2025).  
- **Incentives for DPIIT-certified firms:** Access to **AI Immersion Workshops for 10,000 MSMEs** across 20 cities, subsidised AI-tool licences, and priority linkage to Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY) funding .

### b) CII AI Awards & India-AI Impact Summit  
- **Objective:** Recognise and scale AI solutions that deliver measurable societal or commercial impact; create a showcase for best-in-class AI projects.  
- **Eligibility:** Indian companies (including startups) that have **commercialised an AI-led product** in 2023-2025; DPIIT-certified startups are eligible and receive **fast-track evaluation** [6].  
- **Process:** Call for entries opens in April 2026, with a deadline of 30 June 2026; evaluation proceeds through two jury rounds (June-August 2026) culminating in an award ceremony (August/September 2026) [6].  
- **Benefits:** Winners gain **visibility at the India-AI Impact Summit**, media coverage, and potential **seed-grant or venture-fund linkages** facilitated by CII’s network [7].

### c) Dx-EDGE (Digital Excellence for Growth and Enterprise)  
- **Objective:** Build a digitally skilled MSME ecosystem, drive AI-enabled process optimisation, and improve market access.  
- **Eligibility:** MSMEs across all sectors; **DPIIT-certified firms** receive **priority onboarding** and may access co-funded upskilling modules.  
- **Application:** Online registration on the Dx-EDGE portal; cohort-based training runs semi-annually (Jan 2026, July 2026).  
- **Incentives:** Free access to **digital toolkits**, subsidised AI-software licences, and **grant-linked pilot funding** from the Ministry of MSME [4].

### d) CII-NUS AI Governance Leadership Programme  
- **Objective:** Equip board members and senior executives with a robust AI governance framework and ethical decision-making skills.  
- **Eligibility:** Senior leaders of Indian enterprises; DPIIT-certified startups can enroll **at a reduced fee** and receive a **certificate of completion**.  
- **Process & Timeline:** Three-day immersion at the National University of Singapore, followed by two days of B2B engagements; runs bi-annually (next cohort May 2026). Applications close one month prior to each cohort [5].  
- **Benefits:** Direct interaction with AI policy makers, access to **global AI labs**, and a **CII-endorsed governance toolkit** for immediate deployment [5].

### e) CII CIES Corporate Accelerator & Innovation Programme  
- **Objective:** Provide growth-stage startups with market-access, pilot opportunities, and mentorship from large corporates.  
- **Eligibility:** Startups with **proven product-market fit**, preferably **DPIIT-certified**, and a clear AI/Deep-Tech component.  
- **Application:** Submission of a pitch deck and traction metrics; selection occurs quarterly, with a 16-week accelerator run [3].  
- **Incentives:** **Corporate pilot placements**, **seed funding** via CII’s partner network, and **IP advisory services** (including patent filing support) [3].
