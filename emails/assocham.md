## 1. Eligibility & Application Details (DPIIT-certified startups)

### 1.1 AI-Code Sarathi  
* **Who can apply:** Early-stage AI/ML product teams that have a working prototype or MVP. DPIIT recognition is **strongly preferred** because the portal auto-verifies the certificate.  
* **Required documents:**  
  1. Certificate of incorporation (Private Ltd. or LLP).  
  2. DPIIT Certificate of Recognition (PDF upload).  
  3. One-page product brief + demo video link.  
  4. Founder/CTO ID proof.  
* **Application portal & timeline:** Online form on the AI-Code Sarathi site; applications open continuously and are reviewed **monthly**. Shortlisted teams are invited for a 30-minute pitch within 2-3 weeks of submission [1].

### 1.2 DeepTech Club Launchpad (virtual incubation)  
* **Who can apply:** Private Ltd. companies or LLPs registered in India, **≤ 10 years old**, with a **TRL ≥ 5** (technology validated in a relevant environment) and a functional MVP. DPIIT status is not mandatory but gives priority [2].  
* **Required documents:**  
  1. Incorporation certificate.  
  2. DPIIT recognition (if available).  
  3. Pitch deck showing problem, solution, TRL level, and market traction.  
  4. IP list (patents, trademarks).  
* **Submission & review cycle:** Applications are submitted via the **NASSCOM Accreditation platform**; the portal validates DPIIT status automatically. Review is **monthly**; shortlisted startups receive an invitation for a virtual demo-day within 2 weeks (same source).

### 1.3 AI Data Labs (FutureSkills network)  
* **Who can apply:** Any entity that is **DPIIT-certified** (fast-track enrolment) or a start-up with a demonstrable AI solution. Labs are open to students, professionals, and start-ups.  
* **Required documents:**  
  1. DPIIT certificate (optional but accelerates onboarding).  
  2. Lab-specific application form (includes brief on the AI model to be trained).  
* **Portal & timeline:** Centralised portal announced in the PIB press release; enrolment windows are **quarterly** and selections are announced within 30 days .

### 1.4 Telangana AI Mission (T-AIM)  
* **Who can apply:** Companies with a **validated AI use-case** in health, agriculture, infrastructure, or related sectors. DPIIT-certified start-ups enjoy **preferential R&D grants**.  
* **Required documents:**  
  1. Incorporation proof.  
  2. DPIIT certificate (for priority).  
  3. Detailed project proposal (technology stack, pilot plan, impact metrics).  
* **Submission & review:** Applications are uploaded on the **T-AIM portal** (linked from the NASSCOM AI initiative hub). Review is conducted by a joint NASSCOM-Govt technical committee; decisions are communicated within **45 days** .

### 1.5 NASSCOM Gamechangers Award (AI)  
* **Who can apply:** Any Indian entity (startup, enterprise, academic, government) with a **demonstrable AI solution**. DPIIT-recognised firms get **priority evaluation**.  
* **Required documents:**  
  1. Application form on the official awards platform.  
  2. DPIIT certificate (optional but highlighted).  
  3. Solution demo, impact case-study, and supporting metrics.  
* **Timeline:** Call opens **1 Mar 2026** and closes **22 May 2026**; virtual presentations of shortlisted entries run **4 - 5 May 2026**, with the award ceremony in **July 2026** [3].

## 2. Incentives & Benefits for DPIIT-certified Firms

| Benefit | How it is unlocked | Typical value / mechanism |
|---------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| **Tax holiday (Section 80-IAC)** | Automatic upon DPIIT recognition | 100 % income-tax exemption for up to 3 consecutive years within the first 10 years of operation . |
| **Self-certification for labour & environment laws** | DPIIT status | Reduces compliance cost and time (same source). |
| **Patent-filing rebate** | 80 % rebate on official filing fees for DPIIT-certified start-ups | Accessed via the **Startup India portal**; NASSCOM’s IP-support network can help file (same source). |
| **Fund of Funds for Start-ups (FFS)** | Eligibility requires DPIIT certification | Access to a **₹10,000 crore** corpus; NASSCOM can forward eligible applications (same source). |
| **Collateral-free loans (up to ₹10 cr)** | Linked to DPIIT status and NASSCOM’s partnership with SIDBI | Application through the **SIDBI-FFS** portal (same source). |
| **Seed-grant schemes (up to ₹20 lakh)** | Available to DPIIT-recognised firms via NASSCOM’s incubation programmes | Disbursed after milestone-based review (same source). |
| **Program-specific perks** | - | • AI-Code Sarathi: free cloud credits, AI-tool licences, mentorship track (source 1). <br>• DeepTech Club Launchpad: non-dilutive cash award (₹5-15 lakh), corporate pilot matching, mentorship (source 4). <br>• AI Data Labs: fee-waiver for DPIIT firms, priority access to 30 labs (source 8). <br>• T-AIM: preferential R&D grant rates (up to 30 % subsidy) for DPIIT start-ups (source 5). <br>• Gamechangers Award: fast-track jury review and media exposure for DPIIT firms (source 4). |

**Activation steps**  
1. **Secure DPIIT certification** and upload the certificate to the NASSCOM Accreditation portal (used for all programmes).  
2. **Select the relevant programme** and complete its specific online form, attaching the DPIIT certificate where asked.  
3. **During onboarding**, mention the desired incentive (e.g., “request 80 % patent rebate”) so that NASSCOM’s IP-support team can initiate the process.  
4. **Post-acceptance**, follow the programme’s milestone calendar to trigger disbursements (seed grant, loan eligibility, etc.).

## 5. Open-Ended Considerations  

* **Policy updates (2024-2026):** The Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY) has expanded the **FutureSkills AI-Data Labs** network to 30 labs, increasing the availability of GPU resources; DPIIT-certified firms receive **fee waivers** and **priority slots** (source 8).  
* **Sector priorities:** Telangana’s T-AIM now emphasizes **critical-infrastructure resilience** (energy, water, transport). Positioning the platform as a “national-security-grade” solution can improve grant odds.  
* **Competitive landscape:** Many deep-tech start-ups target AI-based video analytics; differentiators such as **multimodal fusion** and **real-time truth scores** should be highlighted in the evaluation rubric (often based on **innovation depth**, **scalability**, and **social impact**).  
* **Evaluation criteria:** NASSCOM’s juries typically score on **technology readiness (TRL)**, **market traction**, **team strength**, and **alignment with national missions**. Ensure the application includes quantifiable KPIs (e.g., inference latency, false-positive reduction).  
* **Documentation hygiene:** DPIIT portals cross-check the incorporation date and turnover; any discrepancy can lead to automatic disqualification. Double-check that the **incorporation date** on the certificate matches the one entered in the NASSCOM portal.  
* **Post-acceptance reporting:** Most programmes require **monthly progress reports** and a **final demo-day presentation**. Prepare a reporting template early to avoid delays in fund disbursement.
