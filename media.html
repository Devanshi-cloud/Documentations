Introducing ZSure — Reality Trust Center

ZSure is our attempt to build an AI-powered digital trust layer that helps governments, media organizations, law-enforcement agencies, institutions and citizens investigate whether digital content can be trusted.

Through multimodal AI, ZSure is being designed to analyse and verify images, videos, documents and other forms of digital media, helping users move from "I saw it" to "I verified it."
