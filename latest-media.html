On India's 80th Independence Day, Hypotenuse Analytics India Private Limited, a Noida-based, DPIIT-certified DeepTech startup, publicly launched ZSure.in — the Reality Trust Center™ (https://zsure.in), an Indian AI platform built to detect, verify and help people understand AI-generated and manipulated digital content. We wanted [company] to be among the first to know.

"Can we trust what we see?"

That is the question ZSure.in is designed to answer. As deepfakes, synthetic media and AI-generated identities increasingly challenge the ability of citizens, institutions, businesses and governments to distinguish reality from fabrication, the platform analyses images and videos for signals of AI generation and manipulation — moving beyond a simple "real/fake" result toward explainable digital authenticity intelligence.

It has been built with a strong India-first approach, trained around Indian datasets exceeding 3 million data points and assets, supporting a larger objective: AI models that understand India's unique digital, linguistic, cultural and visual environment.

85,000+ Registrations. 2.9M+ Views Before Launch.
