## Key Takeaways  

1. **All-in-One Email + Phone Extensions** - The most comprehensive solutions that reliably deliver both data types are **EmailMaster, Lead411, SalesQL, Kaspr, Wiza, ContactOut, RocketReach, Apollo.io, and Lusha**. They each overlay the target site (LinkedIn or company pages) and fetch contacts from a proprietary, regularly-updated B2B database.

2. **Data Coverage** -  
   * European-focused tools (Kaspr, Lusha) tend to have higher mobile-phone accuracy for EU contacts.  
   * Global coverage (Lead411, RocketReach, Apollo.io) offers broader phone-number databases but may have slightly lower mobile-phone hit rates.

3. **Export & Integration** - Most extensions support CSV export and direct push to major CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive). Some (Apollo.io, Lusha, Lead411) also provide API or Zapier connections for automated workflows.

4. **Pricing** - Free tiers are generally limited to **10-100 credits** per month, enough for light prospecting. Paid plans start between **$39-$59 /mo** for individual users and scale up for teams. Extensions that charge per credit (Kaspr, Wiza) give flexibility for irregular usage.

5. **Compatibility** - All listed extensions are native Chrome add-ons and work in Edge via Chrome-extension support. Firefox support is available for Lusha and Instant Data Scraper; Safari support is rare.

6. **Privacy & Compliance** -  
   * **Lusha**, **Kaspr**, **Lead411**, **SalesQL**, and **ContactOut** explicitly advertise GDPR/CCPA certifications and state that they do not sell extracted contacts.  
   * Generic scrapers (Instant Data Scraper, ContactScraper) do not retain data beyond the user’s session, making them privacy-neutral but also lacking verification guarantees.

7. **User Experience** - Extensions with a **sidebar overlay** (SalesQL, Kaspr, Wiza, ContactOut, RocketReach, Apollo.io, Lusha) receive the highest usability scores because they surface contacts without leaving the page. Tools that require a separate “scrape” button (EmailMaster, Instant Data Scraper, ContactScraper) are praised for simplicity but may need extra clicks.
