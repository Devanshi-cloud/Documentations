## Language & Keyword Guidance  

- **Core keywords** that surface in LinkedIn search: *co-founder, equity partner, AI startup, seed stage, deep-tech, venture-backed, innovation, smart cities, computer vision, deepfake detection, structural health monitoring* [1].  
- Emphasize **action-oriented “you will” statements** to attract do-ers (source 3).  
- Include **mission-driven phrasing** (“building truth, safety & trust”) to appeal to purpose-focused founders (source 6).

## Structure & Formatting Recommendations  

1. **Headline** - Use emojis and a clear title (e.g., “🚀 Co-Founder | Equity-Only | AI Deep-Tech”). Emojis increase click-through (source 21).  
2. **Line breaks** - Separate each logical block with a blank line; keep paragraphs to 1-2 sentences for mobile readability (source 24).  
3. **Bullet points** - Use simple dashes or arrows for lists; they improve scanability (source 23).  
4. **Hook** - Place the most compelling value proposition in the first two lines (e.g., “Build the truth-engine for infrastructure, security & media”) to capture attention before “See more” (source 3).  
5. **CTA** - End with a bold, single-line call-to-action (“📩 Apply now”) and a clear email subject line.

## Tone & Inclusivity  

- **Inclusive language** - Avoid gendered or jargon-heavy terms; use “you” and “we” to invite diverse talent (source 30).  
- **Mission focus** - Highlight impact (“infrastructure safety, security & truth verification”) rather than only technical specs.  
- **Clarity over hype** - State concrete responsibilities and benefits; avoid buzzwords that lack substance (source 10).

## Hashtag & Tag Strategy  

- **Core hashtags** (3-5 is optimal, placed at the end):  
  `#CoFounder #EquityPartner #AIStartup #DeepTech #StructuralHealthMonitoring #SmartCities #ComputerVision #DeepfakeDetection #SeedStage` [2].

- **Tag relevant communities** - Mention LinkedIn groups such as *AI Startups*, *Smart Cities India*, *Computer Vision Professionals* to trigger group notifications (source 11).

## Organic Reach Boosting Tactics  

| Tactic | Why it works | How to apply |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| **Post timing** - Tuesday-Thursday 11 am-5 pm (local) for peak B2B engagement; test 4 pm for late-afternoon boost [3][4] | Algorithm favors early engagement; posts during work-day windows get more views. |
| **Early engagement** - Respond to every comment within the first 60 minutes; ask a question in the CTA to spark replies (source 14). | Comment-heavy early signals increase distribution. |
| **Leverage founder profiles** - Have each existing founder like, comment and share the post within the first hour (source 14). | Personal profiles outrank company pages for organic reach. |
| **Add media** - Attach a 30-second teaser video or a one-page pitch deck thumbnail; reference it in the copy (“▶️ Watch our 30-sec vision video”). | Visual assets increase dwell time and algorithmic boost (source 9). |
| **Encourage shares** - End with “If you know a founder-mindset engineer, please share” (source 3). | Network amplification expands reach beyond immediate followers. |

## Asset Recommendations  

- **Short video teaser (30-45 s)** - Founder(s) speaking the mission, showing sensor footage or a demo UI.  
- **One-pager pitch deck** - Highlight market size, pilot timeline, equity upside. Upload as a PDF preview in the post.  
- **Founder photo** - Humanizes the opportunity; place at the top of the post (source 21).

Reference them in the copy: “📹 Watch our 30-second vision video below” and “📄 See our one-pager pitch deck (link in comments)”.

## Open-Ended Decisions  

| Decision | Options | Considerations |
|----------|---------|----------------|
| **Tone intensity** | *Bold & disruptive* vs. *Professional & measured* | Bold tone may attract risk-takers; measured tone may appeal to corporate-type founders. |
| **Length limit** | Keep post ≤ 1,200 characters (optimal for mobile) vs. longer narrative | Shorter posts get higher click-through; longer posts allow deeper storytelling. |
| **Salary language** | Explicitly state “equity-only” (transparent) vs. omit any compensation reference (focus on ownership) | Transparency reduces mismatched expectations; omission may attract those who assume a salary. |

Choose the combination that aligns with your brand personality and the type of founder you want to attract.
