### Outreach Tips for Professors Without Public Email Addresses  

1. **Department contact forms** - most IIT department webpages provide a generic “Contact” form; include the professor’s full name, a brief note referencing the specific publication, and request forwarding to the faculty member.  
2. **Office phone calls** - call the department office, cite the professor’s recent paper, and ask the secretary to pass along your email.  
3. **Mutual connections** - leverage alumni networks or LinkedIn to identify former students or collaborators who can introduce you.

By targeting faculty whose recent work directly maps onto Hypotenuse Analytics’ three pillars, the outreach becomes highly personalized, demonstrates genuine appreciation of their scholarship, and clearly outlines the mutual benefits of an equity-based advisory partnership.
