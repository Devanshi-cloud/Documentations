## 1. Infrastructure & Structural Intelligence  

- **Bridge-collapse crisis:** 170 bridge collapses and 202 fatalities reported in Indian media between 2021-2025; official MoRTH count is only 42 collapses for 2019-2024, highlighting a massive data-gap [1].  
- **Condition of assets:** 30 % of culverts, 12 % of minor bridges, 8 % of major bridges and 5 % of extra-long bridges are listed as “poor” in the Indian Bridge Management System (same source).  
- **State-wise hotspots:** Bihar (26 collapses), Uttarakhand (25), Himachal Pradesh (17) - the three states with the highest bridge-failure counts (same source).  
- **Government spending on bridges:** ₹5,300 crore allocated for roads & bridges in FY 2024-25, with 82 % spent; an additional ₹33,000 crore from the Permanent Bridge Fees Fund and ₹8,493 crore from the Central Road & Infrastructure Fund (same source).  
- **Railway infrastructure:** Network reached 69,439 route km as of March 2025; target addition of 3,500 km in FY 2026 and 99.1 % electrification achieved by Oct 2025 [2].  
- **Railway maintenance gaps:** CAG audits (2021-2022) flagged a 57 % shortfall in deploying advanced sleepers, >50 % of track-recording inspections missed, and a 400 % surge in locomotive failures [3].

## 2. Surveillance & Video-Analytics  

- **CCTV deployment scale:** Delhi police installed ≈ 250,000 CCTV cameras by Jan 2024, projected to reach 280,000 by year-end [4].  
- **City-level density:** Hyderabad leads globally with 83.32 cameras per 1,000 people (2023 data); Chennai, Delhi and Indore together hold 91 % of all Indian CCTV units [5].  
- **Smart-City rollout:** Over 83,000 CCTV cameras have been installed across the 100 Smart Cities, complemented by 1,884 emergency call boxes and traffic-enforcement systems [6].  
- **Market size & growth:** India’s CCTV market was valued at **$1.48 bn in 2024** and is projected to reach **$5.95 bn by 2035** (CAGR ≈ 13.5 %) [7].  
- **Video-analytics market:** Projected to hit **$7.12 bn by 2030**, growing at **≈10 % CAGR** [8].  
- **AI-powered video analytics:** Expected to expand from **$5.8 bn (2025)** to **$19.3 bn (2032)** (CAGR ≈ 18 %) [9].  
- **Operational challenges:** High false-alarm rates and fragmented data pipelines are cited as major pain points for Indian police and municipal bodies, prompting calls for integrated command-and-control centres (source 9, 2).

## 3. Deep-Fake & Synthetic-Identity Threats  

- **High-profile deepfake scams:** Ankur Warikoo, Mukesh Ambani and Narendra Modi were impersonated in AI-generated videos that promoted fraudulent investment schemes, leading to emergency court orders for takedown [10].  
- **Indore kidnapping video-call scam:** Fraudsters used a deepfake video call to simulate a child kidnapping, extorting **₹1 lakh** from the family [11].  
- **Synthetic-identity fraud impact:** Synthetic identities account for **4 %** of fraud frequency but cause **7 %** of financial losses in India; overall synthetic-identity fraud losses are estimated at **₹2-3 crore** per major incident [12].  
- **Financial-sector exposure:** Banks and fintech firms report rising deepfake-driven voice-cloning scams, with 83 % of victims losing money and ≈ 50 % losing > ₹50,000 per incident (source 10).

## 4. Digital-Arrest Scams (India)  

- **Scale of the problem:** 1,23,672 cases reported in 2024, up from 39,925 in 2022 (≈ 3× growth) [13].  
- **Monetary loss:** Financial losses surged to **₹1,935.51 crore** (≈ $232 m) in 2024, a **471 %** jump from 2023 (same source).  
- **Typical modus operandi:** Scammers impersonate law-enforcement officials via video calls, claim the victim is under “digital arrest,” and demand immediate payment (often via QR codes) to avoid arrest (same source).  
- **Flagship case:** A victim in Hyderabad transferred **₹15.45 crore** (≈ $2 m) to multiple accounts after being told two SIM cards linked to a high-profile individual were under investigation .  
- **Law-enforcement response:** Authorities have blocked > 4,000 Skype IDs, > 83,000 WhatsApp accounts and > 2.08 million IMEI numbers; Prime Minister Modi publicly warned that no agency conducts arrests via phone or video (same source).

## 5. Regulatory Landscape (India)  

- **RBI digital-lending & KYC rules:** Mandate that biometric data (e.g., Aadhaar) can be collected only with explicit consent; digital KYC must be performed via approved channels and stored on Indian servers .  
- **Intermediary Guidelines (IT Rules 2021) & 2026 amendment:** Require platforms to label “synthetically generated information” (deepfakes, AI-generated media) and remove such content within **3 hours** of a government or authorized order, else lose safe-harbour protection under Section 79 of the IT Act [14].  
- **MeitY deep-fake crackdown:** The Ministry of Electronics & IT has issued advisories (Nov 2023, May 2023) urging intermediaries to implement proactive detection and removal of deepfakes; a new rule (Feb 2026) formalises the “synthetic media” labelling requirement [15].  
- **Smart Cities Mission funding:** ₹8,000 crore allocated for FY 2023-24 (≈ $96 m) and a total of **₹48,000 crore** earmarked for the entire mission (≈ $580 m) [16].  
- **CCTV procurement standards:** MeitY’s “Make-in-India” order (Mar 2024) mandates ≥ 25 % domestic value-addition for PCB assembly of CCTV cameras, with higher thresholds once a semiconductor fab becomes operational [17].

## 6. Market Opportunity (India)  

- **Smart-City surveillance spend:** The Smart Cities Mission has released **₹46,787 crore** to the 100 cities, > 90 % already utilised [6].  
- **CCTV market outlook:** Projected **$7.12 bn** market size by 2030 (CAGR ≈ 10 %) and **$5.95 bn** by 2035 (CAGR ≈ 13.5 %) [7].  
- **AI video-analytics market:** Expected to reach **$5.8 bn (2025)** → **$19.3 bn (2032)** (CAGR ≈ 18 %) [9].  
- **Fraud-detection & prevention market:** Valued at **₹22,842 crore** in 2024, driven by AI/ML adoption across banking, e-commerce and government sectors [18].  
- **Potential procurement pipelines:** Integrated command-and-control centres for CCTV with AI-analytics are being tendered (e.g., ₹14 crore Singrauli project, 2024) [19].  
- **Railway capital spend:** Record capex on new lines, doubling, rolling stock and safety works (Economic Survey 2026) signals continued demand for sensor-fusion and predictive-maintenance solutions [2].
