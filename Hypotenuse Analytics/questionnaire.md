# Questions to Ask Dr. Prashantha About the SHM Product

> Tap any question to see **why it's valuable** and what kind of answer to listen for.

---

## 🟢 Start Here — Warm Up

**Q1. "Sir, in your experience, what is the single most common reason a bridge or building fails — and could AI have caught it earlier?"**

*Why it's valuable:* This gets him talking from his domain strength immediately. His answer will likely touch on crack propagation, vibration fatigue, or ground subsidence — all things your SHM platform monitors. Listen for which failure mode he considers most dangerous — that tells you where to focus your AI model first.

---

**Q2. "If you had to explain our SHM product to a government official in one sentence, what would you say?"**

*Why it's valuable:* This reveals how he sees the product's core value — and gives you a ready-made pitch line. Government clients are your primary buyers, so his framing is gold.

---

## 📡 Sensors & Data

**Q3. "Which sensor gives the most reliable early warning — vibration sensors, tiltmeters, or cameras — and why?"**

*Why it's valuable:* The platform uses all three. His answer tells you which data stream to trust most when the AI gives conflicting signals, and where to invest in hardware quality first.

---

**Q4. "What happens to the sensors during heavy rain or extreme heat in India — do they still give accurate data?"**

*Why it's valuable:* This is a very real concern for deployment. His answer shapes what edge hardware and weatherproofing the product needs — and what caveats to mention to clients upfront.

---

**Q5. "The platform uses satellite data (InSAR) to detect ground shifts under structures. How accurate is this in Indian cities — and what are its limits?"**

*Why it's valuable:* Dr. Prashantha has ISRO-funded research on satellite imaging. This is his strongest domain — he will have very specific, practical answers. Listen for revisit time limits and urban signal interference.

---

## 🧠 AI & Predictions

**Q6. "The AI watches CCTV to detect cracks. What kind of cracks can it miss — and how do we make it more accurate?"**

*Why it's valuable:* YOLOv8 is used for crack detection. Hairline sub-surface cracks are invisible to cameras. His answer will guide whether you need specialised cameras (thermal, hyperspectral) or a different AI model altogether.

---

**Q7. "How does the AI learn what is 'normal' vibration for a bridge — and how long does it take before it can start giving reliable alerts?"**

*Why it's valuable:* This is a critical product question. The LSTM model needs a baseline period before it can detect anomalies. His answer sets realistic expectations for clients during the first weeks of deployment.

---

**Q8. "If the AI sends a false alarm — says a bridge is at risk when it is actually fine — how do we prevent that from happening too often?"**

*Why it's valuable:* False alarms destroy client trust fast — especially with government clients who have to mobilise inspection teams. His answer will likely cover confidence thresholds, multi-source cross-checking, and human-in-the-loop validation.

---

**Q9. "Can the AI predict how many months or years a structure has left before it needs repair — and how confident can we be in that number?"**

*Why it's valuable:* This refers to Remaining Useful Life (RUL) modelling — one of the platform's key capabilities. His answer tells you how to honestly position this feature to clients without over-promising.

---

## 🏗️ Real-World Deployment

**Q10. "If we are deploying this on a busy Mumbai metro viaduct first, what is the one thing you would tell us to get right from day one?"**

*Why it's valuable:* A focused, scenario-specific question that will get you his most practical advice. His answer will likely be about sensor placement, baseline calibration, or data quality — all things you need to nail in a pilot.

---

**Q11. "Are there any existing government standards or IS codes that our AI-generated reports need to follow for infrastructure departments to accept them officially?"**

*Why it's valuable:* Government clients will ask this on day one. Dr. Prashantha sits on multiple institutional boards and knows the regulatory landscape. His answer could save you months of compliance research.

---

## ⭐ Closing — The Big One

**Q12. "What is the biggest mistake companies make when building an AI monitoring product like this — and how do we avoid it?"**

*Why it's valuable:* Always end on this. It gives him full freedom to share his most hard-won insight. His answer might reveal a blind spot in your current architecture, a data problem you haven't thought about, or a client trust issue that kills pilots. This one question alone is worth the entire meeting.
