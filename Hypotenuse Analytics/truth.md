## Event-Detection Today Only Raises Alarms - It Doesn’t Tell the Truth  

- **Siloed by design** - Vibration, CCTV and audio streams are processed in separate silos; India’s Smart Cities Mission has rolled out wireless sensors in **more than 400 cities** for traffic, environmental and safety monitoring, yet integration remains piecemeal, leaving operators with fragmented data [1].

- **Threshold traps** - Alerts fire only after fixed limits are breached; a 2022 Odisha train derailment was traced to a missed signal-limit warning, showing how rule-based thresholds failed to stop a fatal accident, while overall rail-safety data reveal that **human-error-driven incidents still account for over two-thirds of accidents** [2].

- **High false positives** - Conventional video analytics generate **up to 90 % false alarms**, and India’s ultra-dense CCTV landscape (e.g., **Hyderabad now has 83.32 cameras per 1,000 people**) compounds the noise, overwhelming operators [3][4].

- **Human-dependent** - Most alerts still require manual review; Indian manufacturers report that predictive-maintenance alerts are often routed to human operators for verification, creating a bottleneck that scales poorly as sensor counts climb [5].

- **No authenticity layer** - Deepfake-driven synthetic-identity fraud has exploded, with Indian banks seeing a **245 % rise in identity-related fraud** between 2020-2023, and RBI guidelines now demanding robust spoof-detection and AI-enhanced liveness checks to curb such attacks [6].
