### Summary of the Discussion

**Aravind's Question**

* Asked whether the company is focusing only on software development that integrates with existing hardware, or whether it also plans to develop and optimize its own hardware for better results.

**Harsh's Response**

* The vision has **two parallel tracks**:

#### 1. Software-First Approach (Phase 1)

* Build an **API-based intelligence platform** that can integrate with any existing hardware, sensors, CCTV systems, monitoring tools, or infrastructure.
* The platform will act as an **AI intelligence layer**, providing:

  * Insights
  * Reports
  * Predictive analytics
  * Better monitoring and management capabilities
* This applies across products such as:

  * AI Structural Health Monitoring (AI-SHM)
  * Surveillance Intelligence
  * Other monitoring systems

#### 2. Proprietary Hardware Development

* Alongside software, the company wants to develop its own:

  * Patented
  * Cost-optimized
  * Prototype IoT sensors
* The goal is to eventually offer a **complete hardware + software solution** and gain a stronger market position.
* Hardware is a major cost driver, often accounting for around **50% of total project costs**, making it strategically important.

---

### Aravind's Perspective

* Emphasized that software alone is not enough.
* AI systems require substantial hardware infrastructure, including:

  * Storage systems
  * Computing resources
  * Cooling and maintenance mechanisms
* Mentioned that major tech companies (Google, Microsoft, Apple) are investing in India partly because of infrastructure advantages and resource availability.
* Suggested that having strong hardware capabilities could provide a significant competitive advantage.

---

### Harsh's Clarification

* Acknowledged the importance of hardware and infrastructure.
* Explained that the company is already aware of the challenges associated with data centers, cooling requirements, and resource consumption.
* However, due to funding and execution priorities:

  * **Phase 1:** Focus on building an agile, scalable software platform.
  * **Parallel Effort:** Begin developing hardware prototypes rather than full-scale hardware infrastructure.

---

### AI-SHM Hardware Vision

* In Structural Health Monitoring, sensors are only one data source among many:

  * Sensors
  * CCTV feeds
  * Other monitoring data
* The plan is to create a **first-generation prototype sensor**, similar to solutions developed by IIT Delhi-affiliated initiatives.
* These sensors would primarily capture critical metrics such as:

  * Vibrations
  * Structural movement
  * Other monitoring signals
* Data from these sensors would then be combined with other sources and analyzed by the AI platform.

### Key Takeaway

The company is **not choosing between software and hardware**. The strategy is:

1. **Build a hardware-agnostic AI platform first** that works with existing customer infrastructure.
2. **Simultaneously develop proprietary sensor hardware prototypes** to eventually offer a complete integrated hardware + software ecosystem.
