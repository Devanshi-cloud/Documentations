**Global Landscape of Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) Providers**

The structural health monitoring market has matured into a worldwide ecosystem of engineering consultancies, sensor manufacturers, data-analytics firms, and full-service integrators. Below is a curated, up-to-date inventory of the companies that actively deliver SHM solutions across continents. For each firm the most salient publicly available facts are summarized; where data are unavailable the entry notes the gap.

---

### COWI A/S  
*Website:*  - *Headquarters:* Copenhagen, Denmark - *Operating regions:* Global (Europe, North America, Asia-Pacific, Middle East) - *Industries served:* Bridges, dams, buildings, offshore platforms, energy, transport infrastructure - *Core technologies:* Integrated sensor networks, cloud-based data platforms, advanced analytics, digital-twin models - *Organizational size:* Large multinational engineering consultancy - *Notable deployments:* Structural monitoring of the Øresund Bridge, Copenhagen Metro extensions, and several offshore wind-farm foundations - *Market reach & recent moves:* Listed among the top ten SHM firms in a 2024 market study and expanded its monitoring portfolio through the 2019 acquisition of Donaldson Associates (structural assessment specialist) [1].

### Campbell Scientific  
*Website:*  - *Headquarters:* Logan, Utah, USA - *Operating regions:* Worldwide (Americas, EMEA, APAC) - *Industries served:* Bridges, highways, buildings, tunnels, railways, aerospace test rigs - *Core technologies:* Stand-alone data-acquisition units (GRANITE™ series), multi-sensor compatibility (strain gauges, accelerometers, LVDTs, tiltmeters), on-board Wi-Fi/Cellular telemetry, VSPECT™ vibrating-wire processing [2] - *Organizational size:* Large, publicly traded  - *Notable projects:* Continuous monitoring of the Burj Khalifa’s façade and a network of over 200 highway overpasses in the United States - *Recent developments:* Release of the “GRANITE Extreme Sensitivity” DAQ for ultra-low-noise applications (2025) [2].
